About PixelPeak Games

PixelPeak Games is an independent game studio making unhurried, atmospheric exploration games. We are currently developing a meditative kayaking sim where you paddle from quiet lakes to open coast, documenting the wildlife and nature you discover along the way.

Kayak Photography Sim

Our debut kayaking sim invites you to explore serene lakes, rivers, and coastline at your own pace, capturing the wildlife and landscapes along the way. More details and screenshots are coming soon.
